# Paweo Privacy Policy

English · [Deutsch](de/) · [Türkçe](tr/) · [Français](fr/) · [Español](es/) · [Italiano](it/) · [Nederlands](nl/) · [Polski](pl/) · [Português](pt/)

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Paweo is an app for keeping track of your pets' care: profiles, reminders, a daily log, a health log, documents and PDF reports. It is available for Android and for iPhone; this policy covers both, and says where they differ. It explains what happens to the information you enter.

**In short: everything you enter stays on your device. Paweo has no account, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet; on Android it does not even have permission to.**

The only exceptions are optional and only on Android: if you use "Scan document" or "Read vaccinations", Google's document scanner or text recognition sends Google technical information about its use (see "Document scanner and text recognition" below). On iPhone, the document scanner sends nothing.

## Who is responsible

Paweo is published by Hakan Sağlam, paweo.support@gmail.com ("we").

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores only what you type into it, in a database on your device:

- **Pet profiles:** name, species or breed, date of birth, weight, microchip number and the registry it is registered with, allergies and chronic conditions.
- **Pet photos:** a photo of your pet, if you add one.
- **Contacts you add:** your phone number, and your vet's or clinic's name and phone number.
- **Reminders:** their type, title, schedule, and when you marked them as done.
- **Daily log:** walks, meals, pee and poop, symptoms such as vomiting or diarrhea, medication and notes, with the time you logged them.
- **Health log:** weight entries, body condition scores, body temperatures, whether an entry was a vet visit, and notes.
- **Documents:** photos and PDFs you add to a pet, such as a vaccination card, lab results or invoices, with the title, type, date and note you give them.
- **Album:** photos you add to a pet's album, each with the day it was taken.
- **Food:** the foods in your house with how much is left, the portion of each feeding reminder and the amount of a meal you log.

This information never leaves your device through Paweo, and we never receive it. We cannot see, access or recover it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create an account or ask for your name or email.
- It does not connect to the internet (on Android it has no internet permission; on iPhone it makes no network connections).
- It does not use advertising, analytics or crash reporting. On Android, the only third-party components are Google's document scanner and text recognition, used only when you choose "Scan document" or "Read vaccinations" (see below). On iPhone there are none that send anything.
- It does not use the advertising ID, your location or your contacts, and it doesn't browse your photos and files: if you add a photo of your pet or a document, you take it with the camera or the document scanner, or pick it in the system's photo picker or file picker, and only what you pick is copied into the app's storage on your device. Album photos come in the same way.

## Permissions

On both:

- **Notifications:** to show your reminders.

On Android:

- **Alarms and reminders (exact alarms):** so reminders arrive at the time you set. You can turn this off in your device settings; reminders may then arrive later.
- **Run at startup:** after your device restarts, the app sets your reminders again, because Android clears scheduled alarms on restart.

On iPhone:

- **Camera:** asked the first time you take your pet's photo or scan a document. The picture stays on your device.
- **Background App Refresh:** iOS may wake the app now and then to set your upcoming reminders, since it keeps only a limited number of them at a time. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn this off in your device settings; reminders are then set whenever you open the app.

The call button next to a phone number opens your phone app with the number filled in. Paweo does not place calls itself.

## Document scanner and text recognition

On iPhone, "Scan document" opens Apple's document scanner, which is part of iOS. It finds the page edges and cleans up the image on your device, and the scanned pages come back to Paweo; the scanner sends nothing to Apple or to us. "Read vaccinations" is not available on iPhone yet.

On Android, "Scan document" opens Google's document scanner, which is part of Google Play services on your phone. It finds the page edges and cleans up the image on your device; the scanned pages come back to Paweo when you finish the scan, and they are not sent to Google. Google Play services may download the scanner the first time you use it. While you use it, it sends Google technical information about its use: device model and Android version, Paweo's package name and version, performance figures, error codes, and device and installation identifiers, for diagnostics and usage statistics. Google encrypts this information in transit and does not share it with third parties (Google's ML Kit data disclosure: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). Paweo itself receives none of it. If you'd rather avoid this, add pages with "Take photo", "Choose from gallery" or "Choose a file" instead.

On Android, "Read vaccinations" on a vaccination document uses Google's text recognition, also part of Google Play services, to find vaccine names and dates on the document's pages. The recognition runs on your device: neither the pages nor the text read from them are sent to Google, and nothing is saved until you have checked it. Google Play services may download the recognition model first. While it runs, it sends Google the same kind of technical information as the scanner, under the same terms. If you'd rather avoid this, enter vaccinations by hand.

## Sharing a health report, sitter sheet or document

When you tap "Share health report", "Sitter sheet" or "Lost poster", the app creates a PDF on your device and opens the system's share menu. The PDF goes only where you choose to send it, for example to your vet or your pet sitter by email. From then on, the app or person you shared it with handles it under their own terms. The PDF is kept in the app's temporary storage, which the system may clear at any time.

When you share a document, the app puts copies of its pages in the same temporary storage and opens the share menu; they too go only where you choose to send them.

## Home screen widgets

Paweo's widgets are available on Android. If you add a Paweo widget to your home screen, it shows your pets' names and today's reminders or daily log counts there, so anyone who can see your unlocked screen can see them. Widgets read the data on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Backups

On Android: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. Paweo allows this only when the backup is end-to-end encrypted with your device's screen lock (PIN, pattern or password); without a screen lock, the app's data is not backed up to the cloud. Encrypted this way, the backup can't be read by us, by Google or by anyone else. You can also move the data directly to a new phone when you set it up. You can turn backup off in your device settings. Documents are included too. Android's cloud backup holds at most 25 MB per app and skips Paweo's backup entirely if its data is larger, so with many large documents, also keep a backup file (see below). The album's photos are left out of the cloud backup, since they would soon fill this space; they move with the direct transfer to a new phone and in a backup file.

On iPhone: if iCloud Backup is turned on in your device settings, iOS includes the app's data (the database, pet photos, documents and album photos) in your iCloud backup, so it can be restored on a new iPhone. Apple encrypts iCloud backups; if you turn on Advanced Data Protection in your Apple account, they are end-to-end encrypted and only your devices can read them. Unlike on Android, the app can't tell whether that is on, so it doesn't limit the backup. We never receive this backup. You can turn iCloud Backup off in your device settings, or keep a backup file instead (see below).

## Exporting your data

In the app, "Back up and restore" saves all your pets' data, photos and documents into one file in a place you choose, for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive or your computer, and can restore them from such a file on this or another phone. The file is the same on Android and iPhone, so it also moves your data from one to the other. The file is created only when you ask for it, and we never receive it. If you protect it with a password, it is encrypted (AES-256) and can only be opened with that password, which we never see and can't recover. Without a password the file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a file replaces the data in the app. The album's photos are included.

## How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a pet (together with its reminders, daily log, health log and documents) in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall (on iPhone: delete) the app. Copies in a device backup follow your backup settings. Deleting a pet also deletes its album.

## Your rights

Under data protection law, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data. Because we never receive your data, you exercise these rights directly in the app: you can view, change and delete everything there. If you have questions, contact us at paweo.support@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
